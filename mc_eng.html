Monte Carlo — delivery forecast

10,000 iteration simulation · Current sprint: 2026.12 (start May 27) · Sprint: 2 weeks

How to read this forecast Monte Carlo simulation runs 10,000 scenarios by randomly sampling your team's historical throughput. The result is a probability distribution — not a single date, but a range of likely outcomes. Use 75% for optimistic planning, 85% for stakeholder commitments, and 99% when you need near-certainty.
